Implemented and installed on MyApple on 16 October 2026. The paired Linux worker is updated and its authenticated TLS endpoints advertise `matchPipelineVersion=1`. Its configuration and checklists were unchanged by deployment. The phone fetched 35 available checklists. The benchmark credential was not used or modified.

## Collector flow

1. Camera capture saves a recoverable draft and opens the comparison sheet. Photo import saves drafts that can be resumed under **Scans vergleichen**. The two-sided capture form opens comparison after saving its photos.
2. Photo extraction supplies readable identity hints. A bounded lookup across actual checklist rows builds candidates; Decisions ranks up to 40 supplied candidates plus unknown, returning five suggestions. This is an additional ranking step, not a replacement for free-form extraction. It does not generate missing parallel membership.
3. The collector compares their front/back photo with candidate year, product, number and finish. Base reference artwork is shown only for base entries; parallels with no exact artwork show a labeled placeholder. Unknown/refusal/transport failures never auto-select a candidate. Manual catalog search works without Decisions.
4. The collector selects the card and an optional existing/new folder. An atomic local save confirms ownership, records the choice and checklist source/date against the photo job revision, and queues a new job carrying the manual assignment. Double taps do not create another card or assessment job. Saving unidentified leaves no claimed checklist slot.
5. The existing assessment and exact-card pricing lane runs after selection. Its photo condition check reports visible damage and inspection limits, not a certified numerical grade. Price evidence retains the existing raw/graded, exact-variant and freshness boundaries. Missing comps leave the value open. **Nächste Karte scannen** returns to scanning.

Folders are independent of checklist binders. Card notes support adding/removing the same physical record in multiple folders without multiplying ownership, checklist completion or collection value. Older libraries decode with no folders; existing cards are not reassigned.

## Persistence and worker boundaries

`OwnedCard.isScanDraft` separates pending selection from ownership. `Library.collectedCards`, checklist ownership and statistics exclude drafts. Suggestions, uncertainty and selection provenance persist with the record; folder membership references stable card IDs. The user's choice rotates the job revision and clears the previous assessment. Older responses are rejected, and conflicting identities on the selected revision are rejected too.

The `match` worker pipeline identifies/ranks, then remains `identified` until selection. It survives restart without entering enrichment. Failed identification remains retryable without starting a full analysis on an unselected match. Existing `fast` jobs retain their previous behavior and do not acquire the new Decisions call. Old servers still support local checklist comparison through the existing analysis response; the installed server supports the dedicated pause boundary.

The context-catalog candidate file remains a review artifact. This change uses the worker's existing checklist rows as explicitly unverified candidate context; it does not publish that artifact, install a source-update schedule, promote collector choices into verified facts, or imply complete card coverage.

## Remaining limits

Exact variant images and reviewed catalog coverage are still the main content gap. Missing cards need search, a later investigation or an unidentified save. Neither the one-card live smoke nor simulator interaction establishes recognition accuracy, physical-camera throughput, grading accuracy or price coverage. A physical scan with the new capture-to-sheet sequence remains a real-device check.
